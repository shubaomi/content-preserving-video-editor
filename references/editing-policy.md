# Editing Policy

Improve watchability while retaining the speaker's argument, demonstrations, caveats, conclusion, and personality. Do not equate shorter duration with better editing.

## Preserve

Remove recording setup, terminal dead air, sustained silence, accidental duplicate takes, and unusable frames. Keep live debugging when it contributes context; flag it instead of deleting it automatically.

## Balanced

Also remove obvious false starts, repeated explanations, and waits that add no information. Keep one complete version of every distinct point.

## Tight

Allow broader compression for platform pacing. Require explicit approval of semantic cuts.

## Coverage Gates

Calculate kept source duration, coverage ratio, first and last source times, unexplained leading/trailing gaps, and every removed interval over 15 seconds.

Fail the complete label when the last used time is more than 10 seconds before source end without an approved reason. Fail automatic approval when a semantic deletion over 5 seconds lacks a cut-report entry.

Preserve source and output time in the EDL for deterministic caption and overlay remapping.
